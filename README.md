## 🧩 理論框架 | Theoretical Framework

### `Asymmetric Threat Resonance (ATR)`
* 探討中俄在缺乏明確協調的情況下，如何透過核訊號的相互強化侵蝕中國「不首先使用」政策的可信度，進而削弱美國在印太地區的延伸嚇阻。
  * *Explores how China and Russia, without explicit coordination, produce mutually reinforcing nuclear signals that erode the credibility of China's No First Use policy and undermine U.S. extended deterrence in the Indo-Pacific.*
* 理論脈絡承接 `Waltz–Sagan` 核擴散辯論、`Yarhi-Milo`、`Lanoszka & Cooper` 的 **「Patron's Dilemma」**。
  * *Builds on the Waltz–Sagan proliferation debate and Yarhi-Milo, Lanoszka & Cooper's "Patron's Dilemma."*
